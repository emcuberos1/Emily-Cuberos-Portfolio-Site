FIU Master of Architecture — Design Studio 4

Florida Room
at Hiss

The task was to design a Florida Room sited between two real, historic Sarasota buildings: the Hiss Studio (Edward Seibert, 1953) and the Hiss Residence — “Umbrella House” (Paul Rudolph, 1953–1954). The new room had to hold its own in conversation with both, while introducing its own identity into the site.

The design lifts the room onto a slender concrete-pier structure, angling the upper volume against the datum line the two historic buildings share — a single canted move that reads clearly in both plan and section.
