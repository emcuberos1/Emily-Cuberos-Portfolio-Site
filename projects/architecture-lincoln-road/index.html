FIU Master of Architecture — Design Studio 1

Lincoln Road
Folly Addition

The task was to design a small pavilion for the eastern end of the Lincoln Road pedestrian mall in Miami Beach. The program called for a shaded area with integrated seating, a place to wash one’s feet, and integrated planters — plus a redesign of that entire section of Lincoln Road, with varied elevations throughout. The design also had to reference an earlier project of mine that turned a painting into a 3D object.
